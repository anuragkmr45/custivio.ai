# Custivio Manager Guide

## Purpose

Managers and approvers review operational status, approve or reject transfer requests, and monitor maintenance, warranties, and reports.

## Transfer Decision Workflow

1. Open **Custody**.
2. Select **Transfer requests**.
3. Review asset, target custodian, and reason.
4. Select **Approve** or **Reject**.
5. Confirm the status updates in the table.

## Review Workflow

- Use **Dashboard** for high-level counts.
- Use **Assets** to inspect asset condition and location.
- Use **Maintenance** to track open tickets and resolved work.
- Use **Audit & Reports** for campaign status and exports.

## Escalation

If records look stale, ask an administrator to check **Status** and worker health before repeating a long-running job.
