# Resource Sizing

## Small

- App LXC: 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM.
- Data VM: 4 vCPU, 8-16 GB RAM.
- Observability LXC/VM: 2 vCPU, 4-8 GB RAM.

## Medium

- App LXC: 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM.
- Data VM: 6-8 vCPU, 16-32 GB RAM.
- Observability LXC/VM: 4 vCPU, 8-16 GB RAM.

Actual sizing depends on inventory count, attachment/file volume, metrics/log retention, report size, worker load, and concurrent users.
