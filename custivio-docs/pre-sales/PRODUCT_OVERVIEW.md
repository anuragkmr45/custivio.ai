# Custivio Product Overview

Custivio is built for organizations that need local control over IT assets, spares, consumables, audit evidence, and stock movement records.

## Best Fit

- Indian SMEs with IT stores, spare inventory, and multi-user custody workflows.
- Enterprises and public-sector teams that need on-prem or air-gapped deployment.
- IT, facilities, operations, and compliance teams tracking computers, laptops, servers, network devices, racks, cables, components, consumables, accessories, licenses, maintenance records, audit evidence, and report files.

## Core Workflows

- Asset registry with tags, serial numbers, models, vendors, location, custodian, lifecycle events, attachments, and QR payloads.
- Stock item tracking with receive, issue, transfer, adjust, balances, movement ledger, and low-stock rules.
- Location hierarchy for sites, buildings, floors, rooms, racks, shelves, and bins.
- Custody assignment, handover, check-in, transfer requests, approval, and rejection.
- Maintenance tickets, events, warranties, priorities, downtime context, and resolution tracking.
- Audit campaigns, scan results, findings, imports, exports, saved filters, and reports.
- Tenant-aware RBAC, audit logs, object storage, worker queues, metrics, dashboards, and deployment runbooks.

## Positioning

Custivio is strongest as private-cloud, on-prem, hybrid, or air-gapped inventory and IT asset operations software. It is not currently positioned as retail order management, accounting, ecommerce inventory, manufacturing MRP, or full ERP.
